About

I am completing an MSc in Software Engineering at the University of Tehran, working with Dr. Azadeh Shakery at the Intelligent Information Systems Lab.

My research is centered on how language and retrieval systems model people: how they infer preferences from behavior and dialogue, how those inferences can drift beyond the available evidence, and how we can evaluate and audit those failures. I am particularly interested in the intersection of large language models, personalization, conversational systems, retrieval, and reliability.

Before focusing more deeply on research, I spent around four years at NeshanMaps building search, geocoding, and ML-assisted systems at production scale. That experience strongly shapes how I think about evaluation: a model is useful only when its behavior can be measured against real users and real constraints.
